Terms of Submission

By submitting an image to Watney Astrometry, you agree to the following:

  1. You own the image or have permission to submit it. Do not upload images you do not own or have rights to.
  2. You agree to let this site to process, host and display the image. The image will be resized/compressed for web use.
  3. No inappropriate content. Images must be astronomical in nature.
  4. Public submissions: If you tick the "Allow others to see this submission" checkbox, you accept that the image and solve results will be shown publicly in the gallery.
  5. Private submissions: non-public submissions will be viewable by a direct link to the submission. Anyone with the link is able to access it.
  6. No guarantees. The astrometric solver may fail for some images. Results are provided as-is.
  7. Data retention. Submitted images are stored for the purpose of solving and display. We reserve the right to remove any content at any time.
